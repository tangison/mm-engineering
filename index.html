Practice Overview

M&M Engineering & Consultancy CC builds hospitals, police stations, electrical networks, and public works across Namibia.

Based in Kleine Kuppe and Windhoek North since 2013, our practice pairs hands-on contracting crews with formal design coordination, EPIC procurement, and calibrated testing. Every contract stands on named leadership and strict compliance under the Labour Act (Act 11 of 2007) and Government Notice 156.
