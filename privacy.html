Privacy policy

How M&M Engineering handles personal information, in plain language.

Last updated: 9 November 2026Applies to mmengineeringcc.com

M&M Engineering CC (Reg. CC/2014/07916), 8th Floor, Capital Centre Building, Windhoek, Namibia, operates this website. We collect as little personal information as the relationship requires, and we do not sell it. This policy explains what we collect, why, and what your options are.

What we collect

  • Contact enquiries. When you email us, WhatsApp us, or use the contact form, we receive the details you choose to send: your name, company, email address, phone number and the content of your message.
  • Communication records. We keep a record of correspondence so that we can answer you properly and keep project history accurate.
  • Basic technical data. Like most websites, our hosting may process standard technical logs (such as IP address, browser type and pages visited) for security and statistics.

How we use it

  • To respond to enquiries and prepare quotations or proposals.
  • To perform contracts and administer projects you are involved in.
  • To meet legal, tax and regulatory obligations that apply to a registered close corporation in Namibia.
  • To keep this website and our communications secure.

What we do not do

  • We do not sell or rent personal information.
  • We do not run third-party advertising trackers on this website.
  • We do not send marketing newsletters without your consent.

Sharing

We share personal information only where it is necessary to deliver a service you have asked for (for example, with a contractor or supplier involved in your project), with professional advisers under confidentiality, or where the law requires it.

Retention and security

Enquiry correspondence is kept for as long as it is useful for the working relationship and for our record-keeping obligations, after which it is deleted. We apply reasonable technical and organisational measures to protect personal information against loss or unauthorised access.

Your choices

You may ask us at any time to confirm what personal information of yours we hold, to correct it, or to delete it where we are not required to keep it. Write to info@mmengineeringcc.com or call +264 61 234 349.

Contact

Questions about this policy go to the office: M&M Engineering CC, P.O. Box 51121, Bachbrecht, Windhoek, Namibia, or info@mmengineeringcc.com.
