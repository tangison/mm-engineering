Electrical & Instrumentation

HV/LV reticulation & 10 kV testing.

Every electrical contract is proven with company-owned calibrated meters prior to energisation.

By M&M Engineering Practice Desk Published November 2026 Category: Electrical & Instrumentation

Whether deploying bulk reticulation infrastructure at Ekuku Township Phase 2 (N$ 13.6M), Kaisosi NHE (N$ 11.0M), Nukwa Military Base, or Okakarara Hospital emergency power stations, reliable field commissioning depends on verified test instruments.

Owned testing suite

  • Major Tech K3123A 10,000 V Insulation Testers (2 units): High-voltage dielectric integrity verification across MV cables, pole-mounted transformers, and switchgear cubicles prior to live commissioning.
  • Hellermann Tyton T1825 Loop / PSC / Load Testers (5 units): Prospective short-circuit current and fault-loop impedance validation up to 1,000 V across distribution boards and consumer feeders.
  • Major Tech K4105A Earth Resistance Testers (3 units): Fall-of-potential substation earth-mat and lightning protection ground resistance logging in dry sandy soils.
  • Fluke 1000 V Clamp Meters, Phase Rotation (8031F) & Polarity Testers: Phase sequencing, balance verification, and RCD trip timing checks governed by Government Notice 156 Chapter 9.

Documentation and client witness testing

Before energising any distribution board, substation feeder, or standby generator set, site engineers verify conductor polarity, insulation resistance across phases, and protective device discrimination curves under load. Signed test certificates, serialised instrument calibration logs, and red-line as-built schematics are bound directly into close-out packs alongside municipal and consulting engineers, ensuring long-term operational reliability for public hospitals, military bases, and regional town councils across Namibia.
