Governance & OHS

The business of good ethics.

Safety, transparency, and statutory compliance are core operating controls across our contracting practice.

By M&M Engineering Practice Desk Published November 2026 Category: Governance & OHS

In public construction and civil contracting, ethical discipline shows up in unglamorous daily habits: clear bills of quantities, valid tax standing, certified lifting gear, and honest site supervision when a trench reveals unexpected rock or groundwater.

Statutory safety as baseline practice

Namibia's Labour Act (Act No. 11 of 2007) and Government Notice No. 156 establish strict engineering responsibilities: mandatory induction, no charge to employees for safety training or protective footwear, weekly fire-alarm verification, and documented COSHH chemical registers before materials arrive on site.

Treating safety as an engineering control, not simply paperwork before tender submission, protects crews working around energized 11 kV switchgear, open excavations, and elevated hospital roofs.

Transparency across public contracts

Across 25 contracts for ministries, town councils, universities, and parastatals, repeat invitations depend on delivering what was priced and passing consultant sign-off cleanly. Named accountability from our Windhoek head office keeps subcontractors, materials testing, and completion schedules aligned.
