Practice Role Guide

What a consulting engineer does.

The planning, technical verification, HAZOP supervision, and site control that keep infrastructure budgets and schedules intact.

By M&M Engineering Practice Desk Published November 2026 Category: Technical Advisory

Construction problems rarely begin when concrete is poured or trenching starts. They almost always trace back to gaps between architectural drawings, structural schedules, municipal water layouts, and medium-voltage cable routes.

1. Design coordination before mobilisation

A specialist advisor checks cross-disciplinary compatibility before contractors break ground. When hospital ward upgrades or vocational campus workshops bring together HVAC ducting, fire-fighting ring mains, backup generators, and data risers, resolving clashes on paper prevents expensive demolition once trades arrive.

Across contracts with firms such as Emcon, Aurecon, Jacobs, WML, and AIJ Project Cost Consultants, M&M has seen how early drawing coordination keeps practical completion predictable.

2. Tendering preparation and BOQ auditing

Public-sector tenders fail on missing statutory schedules, under-scoped provisional sums, or incompatible equipment specifications. Advisory teams verify bills of quantities line by line, reconcile long-lead switchgear against site timelines, and ensure bank confirmations and tax standing certificates align with procurement rules.

3. HAZOP safety reviews and site supervision

Hazard and Operability (HAZOP) reviews identify physical operational risks across substations, pump stations, and industrial pipework before live commissioning. During active construction, regular technical walkdowns verify reinforcement bar placement, pipe bedding compaction, concrete cube strength, and adherence to Namibia's Labour Act and Government Notice 156.

4. Witness testing and recovery audits

When infrastructure stalls, independent schedule audits uncover critical-path bottlenecks, unapproved variation orders, and material delivery delays so works recover quickly. Before final handover, calibrated witness testing confirms that installed assets meet contract specifications.
