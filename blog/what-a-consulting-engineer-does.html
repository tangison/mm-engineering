Consulting

What a consulting engineer actually does

By M&M EngineeringWindhoek5 min read

Most people meet a consulting engineer across a table covered in drawings, months before anything is built. What they are actually hiring is independence: a professional whose judgement belongs to the project, not to any supplier on it.

The definition we work to

Consulting engineering is a professional service that provides independent expertise in engineering, science and related areas to governments, industries, developers and construction firms. Our consulting engineering services are offered through our consulting engineering division, and they carry a responsibility larger than any single project: we are designing and building the infrastructure that is vital to Namibia's social, economic and environmental quality of life.

How the team is built

Within our firm, projects are carried out by teams comprised of a variety of professions and disciplines in addition to engineers and technologists, but the team leader is usually a professional engineer. That is deliberate. Coordination is a technical discipline of its own, and someone must own the whole.

A team might include:

  • Consulting engineers from virtually every discipline and specialty
  • Technologists who implement specific technologies in the field
  • Planners, CAD operators and surveyors
  • Architects and construction specialists
  • Environmental biologists, geoscientists and archaeologists where the ground demands them
  • Economists and property negotiators where the business case needs them

What the engineers actually do

Opportunities within a project range from purely scientific or technical roles to coordinating or managerial roles, and combinations thereof, depending on the type and size of the project. In many cases our consulting engineers coordinate the efforts and activities of other project team members, including other disciplines and specialties, to ensure effective project delivery. A consulting engineer may also find themselves entirely specialised within their own sphere, which is exactly the variety the profession offers.

Why independence matters to the client

An independent consultant has no product to sell you and no contract to protect. Their professional duty runs to the quality of the decision. That is why developers hire firms like ours early, before the design is shaped by whoever arrived first, and why public institutions run their feasibility and supervision work through consulting engineers.

If you are planning infrastructure in Namibia or the wider region, the conversation can start before you have drawings. That is the point of consulting.
