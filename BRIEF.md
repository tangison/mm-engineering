# BRIEF.md — M&M Engineering CC website

**Self-authored, not interviewed.** The human gave direction in chat (hyper-visual,
ultra-minimal, fancy, floating nav, scroll animations, real content only, contact via
WhatsApp/WeChat/email, credit studio.tangison.com) and is not reachable for a live
interview. Answers below are written in the brand's voice from their own site copy
and the chat instructions. Marked per SKILL.md Step 0 fallback rule.

## The eight answers

1. **Vibe** — "Proud. Grounded. Precise. Quietly powerful." References: a total
   station at dawn; the Mathias Mordhorst-grade of industrial editorial; a
   well-kept site office wall.
2. **Scroll journey** — land on the ground we build on → hear what M&M is in plain
   sentences → walk the six disciplines sideways → read the facts that hold it up →
   stand in front of the team and the work at full scale → one door: talk to us.
3. **Energy curve** — calm open, rising curiosity through the disciplines, dense
   and grounded in the facts, one held-breath peak at the team shot, quiet close.
4. **Feeling** — arrival: calm awe. Middle: recognition, then trust. Peak: scale.
   Close: readiness. The ONE moment: the full-bleed team frame under the structure
   with the light following your hand.
5. **One thing no site does** — a surveyor's crosshair with a live coordinate
   readout follows your pointer across the whole page, like the site itself is
   being set out.
6. **Range** — premium-minimal with an industrial spine. Not brutalist, not
   maximalist.
7. **World or scenes?** — Distinct chapters with hard grounds (this is a company
   with departments, not one continuous flight). Editorial, not filmic.
8. **Assets** — the real logo (extracted from the client's own banners), six real
   archive photos from their old slider, three legacy page images, and one new
   photographic set shot to a single grade. No video budget.

## Feeling curve (written before the acts)

1. Calm awe — golden-hour site under a big sky, slow parallax under the type
2. Recognition — held frame, plain sentences naming exactly what M&M does
3. Curiosity — the six disciplines travel sideways, each card landing whole
4. Trust — dense editorial spread: real registration facts, figures, lists
5. Scale (PEAK) — full-bleed team frame, layered parallax, light follows the hand
6. Readiness — everything stops, one CTA, the contact channels, footer resolves

## The peak

"The whole crew walked under the structure and the light followed my hand across
the frame." Lives in act 5. It gets the largest span (2.9vh), the silence after the
quiet facts act, and the only spotlight surface besides the hero.

build, with live setting-out coordinates in the corner."

quiet tail: its last cue holds on a single registration line and nothing else
moves for the last third of the act.
