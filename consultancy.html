Advisory & Supervision

Technical advisory that prevents rework.

Design coordination, tender preparation, HAZOP reviews, and programme supervision supporting public authorities and commercial developments across Namibia.

Six Advisory Pillars

Independent oversight built on site experience.

01 / COORDINATION

Design Coordination

Alignment of civil, structural, electrical, and mechanical drawings before mobilisation, preventing service clashes on active hospital, housing, and substation sites.

02 / SAFETY REVIEWS

HAZOP & Risk Control

Formal Hazard and Operability reviews covering switchgear stations, pipeline tie-ins, confined spaces, and statutory Labour Act inspections.

03 / TENDERING

Competitive Tender Advisory

Complete bill-of-quantities verification, technical schedule compilation, method statement drafting, and statutory pre-qualification checks for public bids.

04 / RECOVERY

Programme Audits & Turnarounds

Independent schedule diagnostics, variation reconciliation, and recovery plans for delayed infrastructure works requiring rapid stabilisation.

05 / ENVIRONMENT

Environmental Assessments

Environmental Impact Assessment coordination, waste-management planning, and remediation audits aligned with Namibian environmental regulations.

06 / QUALITY ASSURANCE

Witness Testing & Handover

On-site quality inspections, 10 kV insulation witnessing, concrete cube verification, and structured close-out documentation for client sign-off.

Practice Approach

Backed by active site builders.

Our advisory work is grounded in real site delivery. Every project team includes architects, quantity surveyors, specialist trades, and supervising staff coordinated under single-point control.
