Statutory Governance

Occupational Health & Safety Policy.

Compliant with the Constitution of the Republic of Namibia, Labour Act 2007 (Act No. 11 of 2007), and Government Notice No. 156 of 1997.

OHS Mandate & Controls

Ten operational protection controls enforced across sites.

01 · Statement of General Policy

M&M Engineering prevents workplace injury and occupational ill-health through well-maintained plant, controlled hazardous-substance handling, active worker consultation, and annual statutory reviews.

02 · Responsibilities & Governance

Overall OHS accountability sits with Executive Management, supported daily by appointed OHS Officers, line supervisors, and worker-elected workplace representatives.

03 · Risk Assessment & 5-Tier Control

Written task risk assessments cover every worker, subcontractor, and public interface, following strict priority: Eliminate, Substitute, Isolate, Engineering Control, and certified PPE.

04 · Information, Instruction & Training

Workers and subcontractors complete site induction prior to entry, receive certified task instruction, and never bear personal cost for statutory OHS training or protective equipment.

05 · Fire Prevention & Emergency Drills

Escape routes remain clear at all times, alarms are tested weekly, extinguishers undergo monthly gauge checks and annual service, and evacuation drills run every six months.

06 · Electrical Isolation & Lockout

Compliant with GN 156 Chapter 9. Unqualified live work is prohibited, portable power tools operate via RCD protection, and maintenance mandates physical circuit isolation and lock-off.

07 · Machinery & Lifting Equipment

Cranes, HIAB lorry loaders, excavators, and hoists carry stamped Safe Working Loads and undergo statutory 6- to 12-month examinations prior to active lifting operations.

08 · COSHH & Vibration Limits

Only assessed chemicals accompanied by hazard data sheets enter stores. Hand-arm vibration enforces EAV 2.5 and ELV 5.0 m/s² A(8); whole-body vibration caps at EAV 0.5 and ELV 1.15 m/s² A(8).

09 · Incident & Ministry Reporting

All near-misses, first-aid events, major injuries, dangerous occurrences, and lost-time incidents exceeding seven days are logged, investigated, and reported to the Ministry of Labour.

10 · Working at Height & PPE

Falls above 2 metres require minimum 1,100 mm guardrails with mid-rails or full arrest harnesses. Steel-toe boots, high-visibility vests, eye/ear protection, and task gloves are mandatory.
