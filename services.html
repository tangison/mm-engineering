Multidisciplinary Scope

Thirteen engineering and contracting disciplines.

Design coordination, procurement, construction, testing, and commissioning executed by our Windhoek teams across Southern Africa.

Delivery Framework

Engineering, procurement, and construction control.

FUNCTION 01

Engineering

Technical planning, specification control, and site coordination integrated with external consulting engineers and architects.

FUNCTION 02

Procurement (EPIC)

Vetted local and SADC sourcing, bill-of-quantities budgeting, transformer and switchgear lead-time management, and site dispatch.

FUNCTION 03

Construction

Direct civil, electrical, and building execution certified under Namibia's Labour Act and Government Notice 156 safety controls.
