Engineering, Construction & Technical Consultancy

M & M Engineering & Consultancy CC is an independent Namibian engineering consultancy and multi-disciplinary contracting firm based in Windhoek. Established in 2013 and registered across Namibia and the Southern African region, our practice pairs practical site delivery with formal design coordination and procurement.

Our engineering and construction teams deliver complex civil structures, medium- and low-voltage power networks, hospitals, police stations, tertiary campus hostels, mass housing, air-conditioning plants and water pipelines for government ministries, regional councils, state enterprises and commercial developers.

Every project runs under named technical leadership—uniting registered engineers, quantity surveyors, technologists and vetted subcontractors—with strict adherence to the Labour Act, 2007 and Government Notice No. 156 occupational safety regulations.

09 / Health & Safety Policy Manual

Statutory Mandate, Policy Statement & Organisation

NAMIBIAN STATUTORY OHS COMPLIANCE FRAMEWORK

Governing Legislation: The Constitution of the Republic of Namibia · Labour Act, 2007 (Act No. 11 of 2007) · Government Notice No. 156 of 1992 ("Regulations Relating to the Health and Safety of Employees at Work") · Presidential Proclamation No. 10 (Part XI, Section 101).

1. Statement of General Policy
  • Provide adequate control of health and safety risks arising from all work activities.
  • Consult actively with employees and safety representatives on workplace health and safety.
  • Provide, inspect and maintain safe plant, lifting gear and calibrated equipment.
  • Control safe storage, handling and disposal of hazardous substances (COSHH).
  • Deliver documented induction, task instruction, supervision and competency training.
  • Prevent workplace accidents, industrial injuries and occupational diseases.
  • Review and update this policy annually or immediately after site/statutory changes.
2. Safety Management & Governance
  • Directors & MD: Hold overall legal responsibility; fund safety priorities annually.
  • Safety Officer: Audits risk assessments, registers, PPE issue, permits and training.
  • Line Managers & Supervisors: Enforce safe method statements and daily housekeeping.
  • Safety Representatives & Committees: Conduct quarterly inspections; committee convened within 3 months of request.
3. Risk Assessment & Hierarchy of Control

Written risk assessments cover routine and non-routine operations, contractors and members of the public using the five-tier control hierarchy:

  1. Eliminate hazardous activity wherever alternative work methods exist.
  2. Substitute equipment or materials for lower-hazard alternatives.
  3. Isolate or segregate hazards via physical barriers and interlocked guards.
  4. Apply engineering controls (LEV, enclosures, ergonomic station design).
  5. Issue compatible PPE as secondary protection where residual risk persists.
4. Right to Stop Unsafe Work

All employees, subcontractors and visitors must stop any unsafe activity immediately if guarding is missing, electrical plant is defective or unsafe site conditions develop, reporting directly to the Safety Officer or Supervisor without delay. No employee is charged for mandatory OHS training or PPE.

INDUCTION & ZERO-CHARGE TRAINING MANDATE

Every employee and site subcontractor receives documented induction prior to site access, verified task competence certification and full employer-funded personal protective equipment.

09 / Health & Safety Policy Manual (Cont.)

Fire, Electrical, Machinery, Lifting & Incident Reporting

5. Fire Safety & Emergency Response
  • Means of escape, fire doors and exit routes kept unlocked and unobstructed at all times.
  • Weekly alarm checks; monthly user gauge/seal inspections on portable fire extinguishers; annual external certification (GN 156 Chapter 3).
  • Fire drills conducted during employment month 1, 3-monthly for night shift and 6-monthly for day teams.
  • Immediate isolation and emergency brigade dispatch for any gas leak or ignition event.
6. Electrical Safety & Lockout/Tagout
  • Complies with GN 156 Chapter 9; zero unqualified work on live electrical installations.
  • Portable tools protected by residual current circuit breakers (RCDs); trailing leads barred from walkways.
  • Machinery cleaning, maintenance or unguarded repair permitted only after electrical isolation, physical lock-off and tagging.
7. Lifting Equipment & Machinery
  • Cranes, excavators, forklifts, telehandlers, HIAB lorry loaders and hoists carry stamped Safe Working Load (SWL) and valid test certificates.
  • Pre-service examination prior to initial deployment and after site re-assembly; statutory examinations at 6- or 12-month intervals.
8. Accidents, Near Misses & Statutory Reporting
  • All injuries, first-aid events and near misses logged on Company Accident Forms and investigated without delay.
  • Ministry of Labour / Health Notification: Fatalities, major injuries (fractures other than digits, amputations, dislocations, loss of sight, electric shock/burns >24h hospitalisation, acute toxic/biological illness), members of public hospitalised from site, >7 consecutive lost-time injuries (reported within 15 working days), and dangerous occurrences (crane failure, overhead powerline contact, scaffold collapse >5m, pipeline incident).
9. COSHH, Noise, Vibration & Manual Handling
  • COSHH: No unassessed chemicals permitted on site; SDS sheets kept on site; strict ban on mixing chemical products.
  • Hand-Arm Vibration (HAV): Daily EAV 2.5 m/s² A(8); mandatory ELV cap 5.0 m/s² A(8).
  • Whole-Body Vibration (WBV): Daily EAV 0.5 m/s² A(8); mandatory ELV cap 1.15 m/s² A(8).
  • Manual Handling: Task, load, environment and individual capability assessed prior to lifts; hoists/trolleys prioritised.

09 / Health & Safety Policy Manual (Annex)

Workplace Welfare, PPE & Site Safety Personnel Register

10. Workplace Welfare, Signs & Edge Protection
  • Minimum 11 m³ room volume per worker; clear pedestrian/vehicle segregated traffic routes and daily waste removal.
  • Guardrails at minimum 1,100 mm height plus mid-rail wherever falls ≥ 2 m (or over hazards) can occur; fragile roofs clearly signed.
  • Potable drinking water, hot/cold sanitary washing stations, lockable changing areas and clean meal facilities separated from dusty work areas.
  • Mandatory Prohibition, Warning, Mandatory and Emergency safety signage compliant with national colour standards.
11. PPE Standards & Contractor Permits
  • Issued PPE includes steel-toe footwear, high-visibility vests, task-rated gloves, eye/hearing protection, harnesses and weather gear.
  • Theoretical and practical fitting training documented before issue; defective or contaminated PPE removed from service immediately.
  • External contractors must submit OHS Policy, Risk Assessments, Chemical Assessments and Liability Insurance prior to arrival, plus obtain Permits to Work for hot, gas, electrical, height or confined-space entries.
