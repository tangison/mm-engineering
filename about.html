About us

A difference that is plain to see.

M&M Engineering serves both the public and private sectors with extensive solutions, honed and tested through years of active experience.

Who we are

M&M is a leading contracting, construction, civil engineering and management company, undertaking the overall planning, coordination and control of construction projects such as shopping malls, multi-storey office complexes and industrial manufacturing facilities.

The carefully balanced combination of knowledge of the local conditions, the innovative approach to engineering and technology, and the experience of working in local and international partnerships, enhances M&M's capability to deliver for its clients. Adherence to stringent ethical standards, and the use of creative engineering and technological tools, make M&M capable of optimally satisfying the engineering and technological needs of its clients.

Corporate social responsibility

With any company's success comes responsibility towards its surroundings and the society in which it operates. Companies often invest significant funds into corporate responsibility projects, but are rarely able to measure their impact. Without a clear strategy and carefully designed processes, spending on charity projects can become money sent down the drain.

M&M has experience with professionalising each phase of a company's CSR: starting from baseline analysis, through strategy and capacity building, to setting transparent processes for project selection and evaluation.

01

CSR strategy preparation

Baseline analysis, strategy and capacity building, grounded in the local conditions.

02

CSR project management and communication

Transparent processes, from selection through delivery to public reporting.

03

CSR project evaluation

Honest measurement of impact, so investment earns public praise instead of suspicion.

Public affairs

Decisions made by public administration have enormous impact on the business environment. Every day, political leaders, lawmakers and the civil service make decisions that can significantly influence a company's prosperity.

M&M's experts build informed opinions on behalf of clients at all levels of government, reaching cabinet members, parliamentary deputies and senators, as well as regional and local administrations.

  • Building relationships with public administration, local government and NGOs
  • Support to investment projects
  • CSR recommendations
