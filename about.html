About us · Reg. CC/2013/6661

Registered Namibian engineering and contracting since 2013.

M&M Engineering & Consultancy CC delivers multi-disciplinary public works, electrical reticulation and commercial builds across Namibia and Southern Africa.

Who we are

M&M Engineering & Consultancy CC (Registration CC/2013/6661, Tax ID 6140420-01-1) operates out of Windhoek from our head office on Erf 454 Rietfontein Street in Kleine Kuppe and our operations desk at 23 John Simms Street in Windhoek North. Since 2013 we have completed 25 documented projects totaling N$ 152,768,801.37 for ministries, municipalities, tertiary institutions and state enterprises.

Our teams pair practical construction management with formal design coordination—working seamlessly with independent architects, quantity surveyors and consulting engineers across Khomas, Kavango, Oshana, Ohangwena, Oshikoto, Omusati, Zambezi, Otjozondjupa and Kunene.

Vision

To provide engineering ingenuity and technical services.

Mission

To be the most sought-after provider of engineering and technical services for Namibia.
