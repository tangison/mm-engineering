About the Practice

Registered in Namibia since 2013.

M&M Engineering & Consultancy CC combines multidisciplinary contracting, construction, civil engineering, and site management under single-point Windhoek leadership.

Company Profile

Multidisciplinary strength under unified site leadership.

M&M Engineering & Consultancy CC is an independent contracting, construction, civil engineering, and project management firm fully registered in Namibia (Reg. CC/2013/6661, Tax ID 6140420-01-1) with operations spanning Southern Africa.

We carry an asset portfolio covering heavy crane trucks, 4x4 site utility vehicles, overhead line stringing gear, and calibrated 10 kV instrumentation alongside multi-disciplinary teams trained for both public infrastructure and private commercial projects.

Our work integrates directly with 13 construction and engineering scopes and technical consultancy, working alongside independent architects, quantity surveyors, and consulting engineers across Namibia.

Operating Principles

Eight core delivery standards.

  • Strict professional ethicsDeliver tailored engineering works that meet client goals while maintaining full civil and electrical code compliance.
  • Value engineering on siteInspect practical alternatives continuously to find safe cost savings and functional gains during execution.
  • Bespoke field deliveryAdapt work methods for remote Namibian sites from Kunene veterinary checkpoints to Zambezi bases.
  • Combined technical coordinationCoordinate architects, quantity surveyors, and specialist engineers under clear programme control.
  • Dedicated Windhoek supportMaintain experienced engineering oversight from our Kleine Kuppe and Windhoek North offices.
  • Direct crisis responseResolve urgent infrastructure failures transparently with fast mobilisation when site conditions change.
  • 24-hour site supportDeploy field technicians rapidly for critical hospital power, water, and municipal network breakdowns.
  • Qualified personnel standardsApply rigorous background checks and statutory training across all permanent and subcontract crews.
